Our Story.

The AI world is moving too fast. Every day, dozens of new tools are launched, but most directories are cluttered with paid rankings and annoying advertisements.

We manually test and verify tools like Windsurf, Cursor, and Bolt.new to ensure our community gets the most up-to-date resources. Our goal is to be the most trusted AI drawer in the world.
